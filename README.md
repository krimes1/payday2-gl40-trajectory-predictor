
**GL40 Trajectory Predictor**

A small PAYDAY 2 mod that helps you figure out where your GL40 grenades are going to land. It draws a trajectory while you're aiming, estimates the impact distance, and shows where the grenade might hit something along the way.

**About the mod**

The predictor automatically activates when you equip the GL40. It shows a colored trajectory, estimated collision points, and a dotted extension when the grenade is expected to explode before reaching the ground.

There's also an experimental counter for GL40 kills made from 40 meters or more, which can be useful if you're working on the long-range achievement.

The trajectory is based on calibrated grenade physics, but it's still an estimate. Actual impacts and explosions might differ slightly.

**Installation**

You'll need PAYDAY 2 and SuperBLT installed.

Download the mod, extract the folder, and place it inside your `<span>PAYDAY 2/mods/</span>` directory. Make sure `<span>mod.txt</span>`, `<span>main.lua</span>`, and `<span>kills.lua</span>` are together in the mod's folder.

Launch the game, equip the GL40, and you're good to go.

**A few things to know**

This is currently  **v1.0 Beta** , so a few things are still being worked on. The predictor might miss certain objects or obstacles, the trajectory can sometimes look a little weird, and the kill counter isn't always accurate.

The dotted trajectory is only a theoretical continuation after the grenade's fuse runs out, not where the grenade will actually travel.

There's no proper through-wall rendering or adjustable line thickness yet. Both are things I'd like to improve in future updates.
